COMPARISON TABLE

Parameter              | Min Heap K-Way | Pairwise Merge
Number of lists        | 3              | 3
Total elements         | 12             | 12
Maximum heap size      | 3              | Not applicable
Intermediate result    | Not required   | Required
Time complexity        | O(N log K)     | O(NK) general case
Space complexity       | O(K)           | O(N)
Scalability             | Better for many lists | Less suitable for many lists
Implementation          | More complex   | Simpler

CONCLUSION
Both methods produce the same sorted output. Pairwise merging is simple for a
small number of lists. The Min Heap K-way approach is more scalable as the
number of sorted files increases because its running time is O(N log K) and
the auxiliary heap uses O(K) space.
